How the outfit checker works

  1. Take a head-to-toe photo. Use good light and include your shoes, so the whole outfit is visible. You can also upload a photo you already have.
  2. Get your AI outfit score. FitCheck rates the look and breaks it down by color match, proportion, and overall balance.
  3. Apply the easy fixes. Read what works and what to adjust, such as switching shoes, changing color contrast, or rebalancing a loose top with a slimmer bottom.
  4. Save the winners. Keep your highest-rated outfits in your digital closet and track how your style evolves.

When an outfit checker helps most

Most outfit doubts come down to three questions: do the colors go together, do the proportions work, and does the look fit the occasion? An outfit checker gives you a fast second opinion on all three before you leave the house, so you are not relying on a mirror and guesswork.

  • Before work or a meeting: check that the outfit looks polished and balanced.
  • Before a date or event: compare two options and keep the one that scores better.
  • When trying a new aesthetic: see how a Streetwear, Old Money, Y2K, Vintage, Minimalist, or Quiet Luxury look comes together on you.
  • When a piece feels off: find out whether it is the color, the fit balance, or the accessories.

What FitCheck looks at in your outfit

Color match

Which pieces, patterns, and color combinations go together, and where stronger or softer contrast would help. A navy top with tan trousers reads calm and balanced; adding a bright third color is easier when it repeats somewhere else, like a bag or shoes.

Proportion and fit balance

Whether volume is balanced between top and bottom. A rule of thumb many stylists use: pair one relaxed piece with one fitted piece, and define the waist when both are loose.

Overall balance and accessories

How shoes, bags, and accessories finish the look, with tips on accessory pairing so the outfit feels intentional rather than busy.

Get the best result from your photo

  • Stand in natural or bright, even light and avoid strong filters.
  • Show the whole outfit from head to toe, including shoes.
  • Stand straight with the camera at about chest height so proportions are not distorted.
  • Use a plain background when you can, so the outfit is the focus.

Why use it

  • A quick second opinion. Get an instant fit check instead of second-guessing in the mirror.
  • Specific, practical tips. Feedback points to concrete changes like shoes, color contrast, or fit balance.
  • Plan ahead. Save top-scoring looks and plan daily outfits for work, parties, workouts, weekends, and date nights.
  • Private by design. Your photos are processed directly on your device.

Good to know

  • The score is an AI opinion and a starting point, not an objective verdict. Your taste and comfort come first.
  • FitCheck does not give size recommendations, take body measurements, or sell clothes.

Frequently asked questions

How does an AI outfit checker rate my outfit?

You take or upload a full-body photo and FitCheck gives the outfit an AI style score with a breakdown of color match, proportion, and overall balance, plus suggestions on what to adjust.

Is the outfit score accurate?

Treat it as a second opinion rather than a fact. Style is personal, and the score is meant to point out color, fit balance, and accessory ideas you can decide on yourself.

What kind of photo works best?

A clear, well-lit, head-to-toe photo that includes your shoes, taken without filters, gives the outfit checker the most to work with.

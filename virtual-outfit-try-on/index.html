Virtual outfit try-on with AI fashion images

Curious how a different style would look on you? FitCheck uses your photo to generate AI images of you in different outfits, so you can explore looks without changing clothes.

Try Outfits On Me

Free on Android · iOS coming soon

How virtual outfit try-on works

  1. Upload a clear photo of yourself. A well-lit, head-to-toe photo works best.
  2. Choose outfits to try. Pick the looks you are curious about, for an occasion or a new style direction.
  3. See AI-generated images of you. FitCheck generates images of you wearing different outfits.
  4. Recreate it with your clothes. Use the look as inspiration, then check your real outfit with the AI outfit checker.

Why try outfits virtually

Changing your style is a gamble when you can only picture it. Seeing an AI fashion image of yourself in a new aesthetic makes it easier to decide what to try next, which colors to experiment with, and what to look for in your own closet.

  • Explore a new style direction before you buy anything. In the app you can also browse aesthetics such as Old Money, Y2K, Streetwear, Vintage, Minimalist, and Quiet Luxury.
  • Plan outfits for an event by previewing a few directions.
  • Get inspired by seeing styles on yourself instead of on a model.

AI fashion image generation

FitCheck's AI image feature turns your photo into fashion images of you in different outfits. The images are AI-generated previews for inspiration. They are not a fitting, so they do not show exact sizing or how a specific garment will fit.

When you have put together a real outfit inspired by a preview, take a photo and use the AI outfit checker to get a style score and tips on fit, color, and balance.

Why use it

  • See it on you. Preview styles on yourself rather than on a model.
  • Fast inspiration. Explore several aesthetics in minutes.
  • Pairs with the outfit checker. Turn inspiration into a real outfit and check it.

Good to know

  • Images are AI-generated previews and do not show exact fit or sizing.
  • FitCheck does not sell clothes or include shopping.

Frequently asked questions

How does virtual outfit try-on work?

You upload a photo and FitCheck generates AI images of you in different outfits, so you can preview styles on yourself.

Does it show how clothes will fit?

No. The images are AI-generated previews for inspiration and do not show exact sizing or fit.

Do I need to own the clothes to try them?

No. The images are AI-generated previews from your photo, so you can explore looks first and then recreate them with clothes you have.

Is it free?

What the score looks at

FitCheck gives each outfit an overall score and breaks it down into categories you can read at a glance, such as color, fit and occasion. Color covers how well the tones and pieces in your photo go together. Fit and balance cover proportion and how the pieces sit with each other. You also get short, practical suggestions, for example switching shoes, adjusting color contrast or adding an accessory.

The score is an AI opinion, meant as a second opinion before you head out. It is not a measurement, and we do not publish accuracy figures. See it in action on the AI outfit checker page.

More than outfit scoring

The app also includes live camera guidance for makeup (AI makeup assistant), hairstyle and hair color ideas (AI hairstyle try-on), a cosmetic skin overview (AI skin analysis), virtual outfit try-on previews, and a digital wardrobe for saving your favorite and top-scoring looks.

What FitCheck does not do

  • It does not measure your body or recommend sizes, and try-on images do not show exact garment fit.
  • Skin and hair features give cosmetic suggestions only. They are not medical advice and cannot detect or treat any condition.
  • There are no human stylists and no shopping inside the app.
  • It is available on Android today. An iPhone version is coming soon.

Your photos and privacy

Outfit scoring is processed directly on your device. A few optional AI features, such as hairstyle previews, AI try-on images and Premium style diagnostics, send the photo you choose to a third-party AI service to generate the result. The privacy policy explains what else the app and Google Firebase handle, including how features that generate AI images work.

Tips for better results

  • For outfits, use a clear, well-lit, head-to-toe photo that includes your shoes.
  • For makeup and hair, face natural light, hold the camera at eye level and avoid filters.

What Colors Go With Burgundy? Outfit Pairings That Work

By FitCheck Team · Published November 23, 2026 · Updated November 23, 2026 · 8 min read

Burgundy goes best with cream, navy, grey, camel, blush pink, forest green, mustard and dusty blue. Black and denim work too if you keep some contrast in the outfit. Below you will find the pairings, the reasons they work and outfit formulas for women and men.

The quick answer: colors that go with burgundy

This deep, muted red has brown or purple in it, so it behaves like a rich neutral. It sits comfortably next to both light and dark shades. In this guide, "oxblood" means the warmer, browner end of burgundy and "wine" means the cooler, plum-leaning end. Names vary between brands, so go by the actual color. A one-glance guide:

Know your burgundy first

This shade comes in two broad versions, and the best partners shift slightly between them.

  • Warm versions (oxblood, brownish, brick-leaning) pair beautifully with camel, olive, rust, mustard, cream and gold.
  • Cool versions (wine, plum-leaning) pair well with navy, grey, icy or dusty blue, blush, white and silver.

Depth matters too. A very dark shade behaves almost like black, so it needs a lighter partner to stand out. A brighter, more saturated one is already making a statement, so quieter neutrals around it work better. To test a combination, hold the pieces together in daylight rather than under indoor lighting, which can shift how both colors look.

Neutral colors to wear with burgundy

Cream and ivory

This is one of the easiest pairings. Cream softens the red and makes it look rich rather than heavy. Try a cream knit with burgundy trousers, or a wine slip dress under a cream coat.

Navy and grey

Navy is a classic, tailored partner and suits offices and dinners. Grey is gentler. Charcoal or heather grey lets the deep red lead without competing with it.

Black

Burgundy and black can look flat if both are dark and matte. Break it up with a lighter element such as a white tee, a cream bag or a metallic accessory. Mixing textures also helps, for example a wine knit with black leather trousers.

Camel, tan and brown

Camel is lovely here because it is clearly lighter. Dark chocolate brown is trickier, because it is close in depth and the two can blur together. If you want to wear them together, add a light layer between them, like a cream top.

White and denim

For weekends, white and blue denim is easy and reliable. A deep red sweater, a white tee underneath and mid-wash jeans need no further thought.

Accent colors that work with burgundy

  • Pink and blush: a tonal look that reads soft and modern. Keep the pink light or dusty rather than hot, which can clash with some deep reds.
  • Forest and olive green: the classic rich combination. It feels most wearable when the green is muted rather than bright.
  • Mustard, gold and rust: warm autumn colors that bring out the brown notes in warmer shades. Use them in small doses, such as a scarf or a belt.
  • Dusty blue and light blue: a cooler, calmer contrast that suits wine and plum tones in particular.
  • Other reds, orange and purple: these work when one shade clearly dominates and the others are small accents. A brighter red is bolder. Plum or berry gives a layered look if it is clearly lighter or brighter than the base. Very dark purple can turn muddy (see the warnings below), and several mid-depth warm brights together tend to compete.

Using color theory with burgundy, simply

Tonal looks: wear it with lighter and darker versions of itself, such as a wine knit, a berry skirt and a rosy-pink scarf. The result looks cohesive and intentional.

Analogous pairings: colors near red on the color wheel, such as plum, berry, rose and rust, sit well together because they share undertones.

Complementary pairings: burgundy is a red, so its opposite is a green-leaning shade. Muted greens like olive, sage and forest are easier to wear than bright ones.

The 60-30-10 idea: use a base color for about 60% of the outfit, a second color for 30% and an accent for 10%. For example, a navy suit (60%), a burgundy knit (30%) and tan shoes (10%). Or a deep red dress (60%), a camel coat (30%) and gold jewelry (10%).

Shoes, bags and jewelry with burgundy

Shoes

  • Black: sharp and dependable, especially with tailored pieces.
  • Tan or cognac: warm and relaxed, good with navy, denim and cream.
  • Nude or cream: lightens an all-red outfit and visually lengthens the leg.
  • White trainers: good for casual looks.
  • Tone on tone: works well when the fabrics differ, such as suede boots with a knit.

Gold or silver?

Gold generally flatters warmer versions, and silver tends to suit cooler wine and plum shades. Rose gold sits nicely in between. Neither is a rule, so choose the metal that looks better against your outfit and complexion.

Bags and belts

Choose a bag in black, tan, cream or a deeper brown to anchor the outfit. A bag in the same shade also looks sophisticated with a neutral outfit, such as grey or camel.

Burgundy outfit ideas by piece and occasion

  • Jumper: with light-wash jeans and white trainers, or tucked into cream trousers with loafers.
  • Trousers, jeans or skirt: with a cream, grey or light blue top. A fitted top balances wide-leg trousers, and a longer top softens a mini skirt.
  • Dress for evenings: add black or tan shoes and gold or silver jewelry that matches the undertone. A camel or grey coat on top keeps it from feeling too heavy.
  • Weddings and formal events: a wine-colored dress with nude or black heels, a camel or grey wrap and matching-metal jewelry. For men, a navy suit with a white shirt and a deep red tie or pocket square. Check the dress code first.
  • Coat or jacket: with a neutral base, such as a grey knit and dark jeans, so the coat is the focus.
  • Work looks: a navy or grey suit with a wine shirt or knit, or deep red trousers with a white shirt and a charcoal blazer.
  • For men: a knit with grey chinos, a white tee under an overshirt with dark jeans, or chinos with a light blue shirt and brown shoes.

Fabric, texture and season

Texture changes the mood of the color. Velvet and satin feel dressy and glossy, while leather reads edgier. Chunky knits are cosy, and cotton or linen blends look relaxed. A single texture contrast, like a matte knit with a shiny bag, keeps the outfit interesting.

In autumn and winter, burgundy works with wool, suede, boots and layers. In spring and summer, go lighter. Try a burgundy linen shirt with white trousers, burgundy sandals with a pale outfit, a small burgundy bag with a cream or light-colored look, or a burgundy skirt with a blush top. Using burgundy as an accent rather than the base keeps it from feeling heavy in warm weather.

Does burgundy suit you? Skin tone and contrast

As a general guideline, warmer shades tend to pair well with warmer undertones, and cooler wine shades with cooler ones. If you are not sure which you are, our undertone guide walks through simple tests.

If a shade does not feel quite right near your face, you do not have to give up on it. Wear it as trousers, a skirt, shoes or a bag instead, or put a lighter color like cream or soft blue next to your face. Treat all of this as a starting point rather than a rule, since personal preference matters most.

Combinations to be careful with

  • Tones too close together: burgundy with dark brown, very dark green or very dark purple can look muddy because there is little contrast.
  • Too many deep shades: a deep red top, dark trousers and dark shoes with no break can look heavy.

The fixes are simple: add a lighter layer such as a cream top or white tee, switch to a contrasting shoe like tan or nude, or add a lighter accessory such as a scarf or a metallic piece.

Frequently asked questions

Can you wear burgundy with black?

Yes, but the pairing can look flat if both pieces are dark and matte. Add a lighter element such as a white tee, a cream or tan bag, or metallic jewelry. Mixing textures, like a wine-colored knit with leather or satin, also adds depth.

Does burgundy go with navy?

Yes, it is a classic combination. It works well for smart and work outfits, such as a navy blazer over a deep red knit. Add a lighter shade like white, cream or light grey so the outfit does not look too dark.

What color shoes go with a burgundy dress?

Black, tan, nude and cream shoes all work well. Black looks sharp for evenings, nude lengthens the leg and tan suits more relaxed looks. Match your jewelry to the undertone of the dress: gold for warmer shades, silver for cooler wine tones.

Is burgundy a neutral?

Not strictly, since it is still a red, but it behaves like a rich neutral in many outfits. It pairs easily with cream, navy, grey, camel and denim. It tends to work best as a base or main piece, with lighter neutrals or one accent around it.

What is the opposite color to burgundy?

On the color wheel, the complement of red is a green-leaning shade, so deep or muted greens such as forest, olive and sage sit opposite it. Muted greens are usually easier to wear than bright ones. For a gentler look, use the green in small amounts.

FAQ

Still have questions? Contact support

Is FitCheck free?

Yes. FitCheck is free to download on Android. See Google Play for current details and device compatibility.

What happens to my photos?

Your photos are processed directly on your device. Firebase use is covered separately in our Privacy Policy.

How does the score work?

FitCheck reads your photo and gives a style score with notes on fit, color coordination and overall balance. Treat it as a second opinion, not a rulebook.

What photo should I use?

A clear, well-lit photo of your full outfit, shoes included. For makeup and hair, face natural light. Keep the background simple and skip color filters.

How does FitCheck compare with YouCam Makeup, Acloset and Whering?

They do different jobs. YouCam Makeup edits and retouches photos, Acloset and Whering organize the clothes you own, and FitCheck scores the outfit you are wearing and gives makeup and hair feedback. Read the side-by-side pages: FitCheck vs YouCam Makeup, FitCheck vs Acloset and FitCheck vs Whering.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. FitCheck is on Google Play now, and iOS is coming soon.

About FitCheck & data use

FitCheck is an AI style assistant for Android that rates outfits, makeup and hair from photos you upload, and can generate AI style preview images. It never generates nude, sexual or intimate imagery; uploading such content or photos of others without consent is prohibited (Terms). This website uses Google Ads Keyword Planner and Search Console only for keyword research on its own content; that research uses no user photos or personal data (Privacy Policy).

About FitCheck AI

FitCheck AI is a free Android app that gives you a quick, friendly second opinion on how you look before you head out. Take or upload a photo and the app gives an outfit score with short suggestions on color, fit and balance. It also covers makeup, hair and skin ideas, and lets you save your favorite looks.

Why FitCheck exists

Getting dressed should not feel like guesswork. FitCheck is built to give clear, kind feedback you can act on in a minute, such as swapping a pair of shoes or adjusting color contrast, without replacing your own taste.

What the app does

  • AI outfit checker: an outfit score with notes on color match, fit and balance.
  • AI makeup assistant: live camera guidance and feedback on makeup looks.
  • AI hairstyle try-on: hairstyle and hair color ideas on your own photo.
  • AI skin analysis: a cosmetic skin overview from the camera.
  • Virtual outfit try-on: AI-generated previews of you in different outfits.
  • Digital wardrobe: save and organize your favorite and top-scoring looks.

See the steps on the How it works page.

What it does not do

Scores are AI opinions, not measurements. FitCheck does not measure your body or recommend sizes, does not give medical or skincare diagnoses, and has no human stylists or in-app shopping. It is available on Android today, and an iPhone version is coming soon.

Photos and privacy

Outfit scoring is processed directly on your device. A few optional AI features, such as hairstyle previews, AI try-on images and Premium style diagnostics, send the photo you choose to a third-party AI service to generate the result. Read the full Privacy Policy for what the app and Google Firebase handle, and the Terms of Service for acceptable use.

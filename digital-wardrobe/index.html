How the digital wardrobe works

  1. Save your looks. Keep your favorite and highest-rated outfits in your digital closet.
  2. Organize them. Sort clothes and outfits by season or occasion.
  3. Plan ahead. Plan daily outfits for work, parties, workouts, casual weekends, and date nights.
  4. Track your style. Look back at your top-rated outfits and see how your style evolves.

How to organize your wardrobe so you actually use it

  1. Edit first. Remove what does not fit or you have not worn in a year.
  2. Group by occasion and season. Work, weekend, events, and workout clothes; then warm and cold weather.
  3. Find your core outfits. Save the combinations you reach for and that score well.
  4. Fill real gaps only. If three saved outfits need the same missing piece, that is the one worth buying.

Build a capsule wardrobe

A capsule wardrobe is a small set of versatile pieces that mix and match. Start with a neutral base (for example navy, gray, cream, or black), add two or three accent colors, and choose pieces that work at least three ways. Saving your outfits makes it easy to see which pieces earn their place.

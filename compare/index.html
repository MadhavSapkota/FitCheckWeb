How these comparisons are made

Each page compares FitCheck with one other app, feature by feature. Details about the other app come only from its own Google Play listing, and each page links to that listing and shows the date it was checked.

We do not score, rank or review the other apps, and every page says plainly when the other app is the better choice. FitCheck is not affiliated with any of the apps compared here.

Frequently asked questions

What is FitCheck best at?

FitCheck gives feedback on the look you are wearing. Take or upload an outfit photo to get an AI outfit score with a breakdown of color match, proportion and balance, plus feedback on makeup and hair and AI try-on previews.

Is FitCheck a closet app like Acloset or Whering?

Partly. FitCheck has a digital closet for saving and organizing looks and planning outfits, but its main job is scoring and improving an outfit from a photo. Acloset and Whering are built around cataloging every item you own.

Can I use FitCheck together with another app?

Yes. Many of these apps do different jobs, so you can keep your wardrobe inventory or photo editor and use FitCheck for a second opinion on an outfit before you leave.

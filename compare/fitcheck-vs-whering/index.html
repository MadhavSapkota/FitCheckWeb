FitCheck vs Whering

Whering is a social digital closet that helps you organize your clothes and see how you wear them. FitCheck gives you an AI score and feedback on the outfit you have on. Here is how they differ.

Try FitCheck

Free on Android

The short answer

Choose Whering if you want to digitize your closet and study your habits. Its Google Play listing describes adding clothes from a large item database or your own photos, statistics such as cost-per-wear and wear rate, packing lists, and seeing friends' closets.

Choose FitCheck if you want to know whether an outfit works. You take one full-body photo and get an AI outfit score with a breakdown of color match, proportion and balance, plus tips to improve it.

Choose Whering if

  • You want a full closet inventory. Whering is built to hold every item you own and show how often you wear it.
  • You want wear statistics. Its listing describes cost-per-wear, wear rate and color palette insights.
  • You want social styling. You can see friends' closets and share outfits. FitCheck has no social feed.

Choose FitCheck if

  • You want a verdict on an outfit. Get an AI score and specific fixes for the look you are wearing right now.
  • You do not want setup work. One full-body photo is enough. You do not need to add your clothes first.
  • You want more than clothes. FitCheck also covers makeup, hairstyle and skin tone feedback, plus AI try-on previews.

Frequently asked questions

Is FitCheck an alternative to Whering?

It depends on what you need. Whering is built for organizing your whole closet and tracking how you wear it. FitCheck is built for scoring and improving the outfit you are wearing.

Does Whering rate outfits?

Whering's Google Play listing does not mention an outfit score. It describes closet organization, outfit creation and planning, and style statistics.

Does FitCheck track cost-per-wear?

No. FitCheck keeps track of your highest-rated outfits and how your style changes over time. Whering's listing describes cost-per-wear and wear rate statistics.

Can I use both apps?

Yes. Some people keep their closet inventory in one app and use FitCheck for a second opinion on the outfit they put together.

FitCheck vs Fits

Fits is an outfit planner and closet app for tracking what you wear. FitCheck starts from a photo of the outfit you have on and tells you how well it works. Here is how they differ.

Try FitCheck

Free on Android

The short answer

Choose Fits if you want to log and plan outfits. Its Google Play listing describes digitizing your closet, creating outfits by swiping through your clothes, mood boards, custom tags, a calendar and an AI stylist chat.

Choose FitCheck if you want feedback on a finished look. Take one full-body photo and get an AI outfit score with tips on color match, fit balance and accessories, plus makeup, hair and skin tone feedback.

Choose Fits if

  • You want a planner and log. Fits is built to track outfits, plan them on a calendar and look back on past fits.
  • You like mood boards and tags. Its listing describes custom tags, filtering and mood boards for organizing outfits.
  • You want to chat with an AI stylist. Fits lists an AI stylist chat built around your closet.

Choose FitCheck if

  • You want a verdict on an outfit. Get an AI score and specific fixes for the look you are wearing right now.
  • You do not want setup work. One full-body photo is enough. You do not need to add your clothes first.
  • You want more than clothes. FitCheck also covers makeup, hairstyle and skin tone feedback, plus AI try-on previews.

Frequently asked questions

Is FitCheck an alternative to Fits?

It depends on what you need. Fits is built for tracking and planning outfits from your closet. FitCheck is built for scoring and improving the outfit you are wearing.

Does Fits rate outfits?

Fits' Google Play listing does not mention an outfit score. It describes outfit tracking, a closet, a calendar and an AI stylist chat.

Do both apps have an AI stylist?

Both use AI. FitCheck gives an AI outfit score with styling tips, and Fits lists an AI stylist chat built around your closet.

Can I use both apps?

Yes. Some people plan outfits in a closet app and use FitCheck for a second opinion before they leave.

FitCheck vs YouCam Makeup

YouCam Makeup is a photo and selfie editor with virtual makeup. FitCheck is a feedback app that scores your real outfit and gives tips on makeup and hair. Here is how they differ.

Try FitCheck

Free on Android

Editorial note. Written by the FitCheck Team. FitCheck makes one of the apps compared here, so read this as a vendor comparison. Details about other apps come from their own Google Play listings and were not tested by us; FitCheck details come from our own app.

The short answer

Choose YouCam Makeup if you want to edit a photo: retouch your face, reshape features, change your hair color or create AI images and videos. Its Google Play listing describes itself as a face editor and makeup app.

Choose FitCheck if you want advice on your real look before you go out: an AI outfit score, a breakdown of color match and balance, and feedback on your makeup and hair. FitCheck does not retouch or reshape photos.

Choose YouCam Makeup if

  • You want to edit photos. Retouching, face shaping and body editing are YouCam Makeup features. FitCheck does not edit your photos.
  • You want a big preset library. Its listing mentions 250+ preset hairstyles and 100+ outfit styles to try on.

Choose FitCheck if

  • You want an honest second opinion. FitCheck scores the outfit you are wearing and explains what works and what to change.
  • You care about the whole look. Outfit, makeup, hair and skin tone feedback sit in one app, so the advice covers head to toe.
  • You want to reuse good outfits. Save your top-scoring looks in the digital closet and plan outfits ahead.

Frequently asked questions

Is FitCheck a photo editor like YouCam Makeup?

No. FitCheck does not retouch, slim or reshape photos. It gives feedback on your real outfit, makeup and hair, and can generate AI previews of you in a new hairstyle or outfit.

Do both apps have virtual makeup try-on?

Yes. FitCheck lets you preview makeup looks such as lipstick on your face with the live camera. YouCam Makeup's Google Play listing describes live and photo makeup try-on with filters for lipstick, foundation, blush and more.

Which app rates my outfit?

FitCheck. Take or upload a full-body photo and you get an AI outfit score with a breakdown of color match, proportion and overall balance. YouCam Makeup's listing does not mention an outfit score.

Are FitCheck and YouCam Makeup free?

Both are free to download on Google Play, and both listings show ads and in-app purchases. Prices change, so check each listing for current details.

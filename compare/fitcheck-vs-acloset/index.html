FitCheck vs Acloset

Acloset is a digital wardrobe: you add your clothes and an AI stylist suggests what to wear. FitCheck starts from a photo of the outfit you have on and tells you how well it works. Here is how they differ.

Try FitCheck

Free on Android

The short answer

Choose Acloset if you want a full inventory of your clothes. Its Google Play listing describes adding items by photo or online search, tracking purchase cost and cost-per-wear, and daily outfit suggestions based on the weather and your schedule.

Choose FitCheck if you want feedback on a finished outfit. You take one full-body photo and get an AI outfit score with tips on color match, fit balance and accessories. There is no need to photograph every item first.

Choose Acloset if

  • You want to catalog everything. Acloset is built to hold your whole wardrobe item by item, with purchase and wear tracking.
  • You want weather-based ideas. Its listing describes daily outfit suggestions based on the weather and your schedule.
  • You like a style community. You can browse other users' wardrobes for inspiration. FitCheck has no social feed.

Choose FitCheck if

  • You want a verdict on an outfit. Get an AI score and specific fixes for the look you are wearing right now.
  • You do not want setup work. One full-body photo is enough. You do not need to add your clothes first.
  • You want more than clothes. FitCheck also covers makeup, hairstyle and skin tone feedback, plus AI try-on previews.

We compared what each app says about itself on Google Play. We did not score, rank or test Acloset, and features, prices and limits change, so check both listings before you decide.

Frequently asked questions

Is FitCheck an alternative to Acloset?

It depends on what you need. If you want a full item-by-item wardrobe inventory with cost-per-wear, Acloset is built for that. If you want a score and feedback on the outfit you are wearing, FitCheck is built for that.

Does FitCheck have a digital closet?

Yes. You can save favorite and top-scoring looks, organize clothes by season or occasion, build a capsule wardrobe and plan outfits ahead. It does not track purchase costs or cost-per-wear.

Does Acloset rate outfits?

Acloset's Google Play listing does not mention an outfit score. It describes an AI stylist you can ask fashion questions, such as whether pieces match.

Can I use both apps?

Yes. Some people keep their wardrobe inventory in a closet app and use FitCheck for a second opinion on the outfit they put together.

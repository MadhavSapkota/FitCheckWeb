Frequently asked questions

Which style app is best?

It depends on the job. For an AI score on the outfit you are wearing, plus makeup, hair and skin feedback in one app, FitCheck. For a full wardrobe inventory, a closet app such as Acloset, Whering, Alta or Fits. For photo editing, YouCam Makeup.

Why does the table say "Not mentioned"?

Details about the other apps come only from their Google Play listings. If a listing does not describe a feature, we write "Not mentioned" because the feature may still exist in the app.

Is FitCheck better than closet apps?

They do different jobs. Closet apps track what you own. FitCheck scores and improves the outfit you are wearing, and it has a lighter digital closet for saving looks.

Can I use more than one app?

Yes. Many people keep a closet inventory in one app and use FitCheck for a second opinion before they go out.

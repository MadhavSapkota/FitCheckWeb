What hairstyle suits me?

Face shape is a helpful starting point, but hair texture, how much styling time you want, and your features matter just as much. Some widely used guidelines:

  • Round faces: length past the chin, side parts, and soft layers add vertical lines.
  • Square faces: soft waves and layers around the jaw soften strong angles.
  • Long faces: curtain bangs and volume at the sides balance length.
  • Oval faces: most cuts work, so choose based on texture and upkeep.

Guidelines only go so far. Seeing the hairstyle on your own face is the fastest way to know.

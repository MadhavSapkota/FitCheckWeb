Skin tone vs. undertone

Skin tone is how light or deep your skin is. Undertone is the color beneath the surface: warm (golden or peachy), cool (pink or rosy), or neutral (a mix). Undertone matters more than tone when choosing foundation and makeup shades.

  • Vein check: greenish veins often suggest warm, bluish suggest cool, and a mix suggests neutral.
  • Jewelry check: if gold flatters you more, you may be warm; if silver does, you may be cool.

Use your results for makeup

Once you know your undertone, match foundation along the jawline in daylight, and choose blush and lip shades in the same temperature family. Then get feedback on the finished look with the AI makeup assistant.

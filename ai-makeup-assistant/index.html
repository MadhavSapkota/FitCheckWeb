How the makeup assistant works

  1. Face the light. Sit facing natural light with the camera at eye level and no beauty filters.
  2. Get AI makeup feedback. Snap a close-up and FitCheck gives beauty feedback on your look, whether it is a natural everyday glow or full glam.
  3. Try looks live. Use the live camera makeup assist to preview and guide your makeup on your own face.
  4. Adjust one thing at a time. Use the feedback to tweak intensity, color, or placement until the look suits the occasion.

AI makeup feedback: what suits you

The right makeup depends on your features, your undertone, the lighting, and the occasion. Instead of guessing, take a clear close-up and get feedback on how the look comes together, then adjust one thing at a time: the intensity of blush, the depth of a lip color, or how defined the eyes are.

  • Everyday looks: keep base light and let one feature, like brows or a tinted lip, lead.
  • Evening and glam: balance a bold eye with a softer lip, or a bold lip with simpler eyes.
  • Matching your undertone: warm undertones often suit peach, coral, and golden shades; cool undertones often suit rose, berry, and silvery shades. Neutral undertones can usually wear both.

Live virtual makeup try-on

Trying a new lipstick or eye look is easier when you can see it before you commit. FitCheck's makeup assist works with your live camera, so you can preview looks on your own face and get guidance while you apply.

For the most useful preview, use even, natural light: warm indoor bulbs can make shades look more orange, and strong overhead light exaggerates shadows.
